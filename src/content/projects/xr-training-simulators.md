10+ VR, AR and MR training simulators for Globo, Gerdau, Aperam and Usiminas, including defensive driving and heavy machinery with high-precision physics.

Each simulator reproduces a real training scenario, so trainees practice procedures and hazards in a safe environment before they face them on site.

## What I did

Gameplay and interaction programming in Unity and C#, vehicle and machinery physics, and performance work for standalone VR headsets.
